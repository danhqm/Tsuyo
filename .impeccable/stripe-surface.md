# Stripe checkout extension

Mode: Operate. Audience: customers completing a purchase and the verified store owner checking Stripe.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Payment status must follow verified Stripe data; sample payments remain isolated from stock and real orders.
OWN-WORLD: Inherit the existing bone/ink palette, upright Barlow Condensed and Manrope, square controls and flat commerce layout. No system or asset changes.
STORY: Customers select a size, review their bag, enter delivery details, see an authoritative MYR quote, pay with Stripe and return to verified confirmation. The owner can run a protected sandbox checkout before opening public sales.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Checkout retains its two-column form and order summary. Payments extends the existing management tabs with connection status, sample-size selection and one test action.
FORM: Local extension of the incumbent commerce surfaces; no new concept tournament or seed applies.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Constraints: test mode only; draft catalog and unconfigured delivery keep real purchases closed. The sample bag gets an explicit, isolated sandbox checkout: one test email field, a sandbox acknowledgement and an automatic server-priced summary. No delivery fee, promo, real order or inventory mutation is implied. Existing imagery and fonts remain unchanged. Browser control tooling is not exposed in this session, so automated render captures are unavailable. Review source/flow evidence at that explicit scope; do not claim a new visual browser pass.
