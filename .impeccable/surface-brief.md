# Tsuyo storefront

## Scope and visitor mode
Persuade and shop: a complete gymwear storefront, product catalog, product detail, persistent bag, wishlist, search, and checkout preview.

## Direction
Industrial training editorial, derived from the user's brief and local references. Quiet bone-colored commerce surfaces surround dramatic black-and-white campaigns, with a burnt vermilion action color. The Impeccable launcher is unavailable in this sandbox, including concept-seed. The user's pinned aesthetic supplies the direction; implementation proceeds directly in code.

## First viewport
Compact cream navigation with a condensed TSUYO wordmark; cinematic monochrome campaign with a three-line BUILT THROUGH EFFORT headline, direct collection CTA, visible campaign identity, and a restrained scroll affordance. No italic typography. The campaign image owns the screen.

## Visitor path
Campaign → concise brand statement → four-product featured collection → men's/women's campaigns → pinned mindset image expansion → newsletter and oversized footer wordmark. Shopping routes offer search, category, sorting, sizing and bag management.

## Signature interaction
The hero sticks under navigation while its image slowly expands and its text recedes. The mindset section begins as a framed gym photograph, then expands to the entire viewport while text shifts between two chapters, YOU VS. YOU and ONE MORE REP. Hovering campaign imagery summons a small contextual cursor; the operating-system pointer remains visible. Product photography zooms on hover, and quick add remains available on touch.

## Motion grammar
Lenis smooth scrolling for desktop, GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubbed images and text, CSS clip-path and staggered product arrival. Native scrolling on touch. Content visible by default and reduced-motion alternatives for every effect.

## Honest risks and limitations
Catalog values are illustrative. No live payment, inventory, email delivery, or shipping service has been connected. Seedance video generation was rejected for lack of credits; a still-image hero supports later optional video.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A training editorial whose dramatic campaigns earn attention and whose quiet product grid makes choosing a piece easy.

OWN-WORLD: Bone #f1f0eb, near-black #1a1a18, graphite greys, and vermilion #e85d39. Condensed Barlow headlines, Manrope shopping controls, square product fields and actions, fine borders, and monochrome photography.

STORY: Understand this is gymwear, feel the private pursuit of strength, browse the core pieces, then choose a size and build a rotation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream header above a full-width gym portrait; a left-aligned three-line 120px desktop headline balances the athlete on the right. Cream collection action below readable supporting copy. Mobile preserves the headline and direct action over a shifted portrait crop.

FORM: User-pinned industrial gymwear editorial. No seed key is available: concept-seed failed because the bundled engine is not installed. This is a direct code build of the requested aesthetic, not a comp-approved reproduction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
